# Narrative Bridge Grammar

A bridge must explain why the next evidence is necessary.

- `LIMIT_BRIDGE`: the prior evidence cannot answer the next question.
- `CONTRAST_BRIDGE`: another place/sample gives a meaningfully different result.
- `METHOD_BRIDGE`: the same question is tested by a different method.
- `SCALE_BRIDGE`: individual -> group -> region -> present-day genome.
- `REVERSAL_BRIDGE`: new dating, contamination, attribution, or comparison changes the interpretation.

A bridge should usually be one or two sentences. It must not invent causality.

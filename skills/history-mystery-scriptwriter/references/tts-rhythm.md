# Korean TTS Rhythm

- Prefer one major idea per sentence.
- Mix short and medium sentences; avoid a wall of identical sentence lengths.
- Use short lines for a real turn: "이름은 이어집니다. 하지만 같은 사람은 아닙니다."
- Avoid repeated academic subjects such as 연구진은 / 연구는 / 논문은.
- Avoid excessive parentheses and slash-separated alternatives.
- Place difficult terms next to their consequence in plain Korean.
- Numbers should be spoken with what they change, not as isolated statistics.
- Do not add rhetorical filler only to create pauses.

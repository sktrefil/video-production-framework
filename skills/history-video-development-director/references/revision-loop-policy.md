# Revision Loop Policy

Default maximum development rounds: 3.

Round means a meaningful writer/director loop after initial preflight.

- Round 1: repair obvious directability and bridge problems.
- Round 2: repair sequence-level rhythm and abstraction repetition.
- Round 3: resolve remaining structural conflict or return BLOCKED to Agent1.

Do not use repeated micro-rewrites to disguise a structural failure.
Do not regenerate TTS during development rounds.

# Camera Tension Grammar

Camera movement exists to shape attention and tension.

## Tension Functions
- BUILD: increase anticipation/pressure
- HOLD: sustain without stalling
- RELEASE: controlled decompression
- REVEAL: expose new information
- REDIRECT: move viewer attention
- HANDOFF: pass energy into the next clip

## Speed Profile
Prefer meaningful change:
- accelerate -> reveal -> decelerate
- follow -> parallax hit -> focus settle
- controlled move -> short hold -> exit impulse

Avoid constant uniform slow movement unless the story specifically needs it.

## Energy Continuity
Track:
- camera_energy_in/out
- motion_vector_in/out
- entry_speed / exit_speed
- entry_orientation / exit_orientation
- visual_focus_in/out

An energy drop between clips requires a deliberate narrative reason.

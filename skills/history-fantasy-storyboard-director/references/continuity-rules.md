# Continuity Rules

## Modes
- BIBLE_MATCH
- EXIT_MATCH
- CONTINUATION
- STORY_CUT

## Four Continuities
1. Narrative continuity
2. Spatial continuity
3. Camera continuity
4. Tension/Energy continuity

## Baton Pass
A prior clip may hand off:
- subject
- eyeline
- motion direction
- object
- foreground shape
- light source
- doorway/opening
- silhouette
- sound cue

Do not use vague labels such as "smooth transition" without naming the baton being passed.

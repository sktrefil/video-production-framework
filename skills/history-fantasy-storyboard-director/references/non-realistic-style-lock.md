# NON_REALISTIC_STYLE_LOCK

## Absolute rule
Final HISTORY_MYSTERY LONGFORM imagery is **NON_REALISTIC_STYLIZED**.

This is not an optional art preference. It is a production invariant.

## Why
Photorealistic historical reconstruction creates high verification cost while contributing relatively little information in a fast-moving explanatory video. Unknown details also risk being mistaken for documented fact.

The project therefore uses stylization to:
- reduce false certainty;
- compress complex evidence into readable visual events;
- allow strong camera and spatial transformation;
- maximize immediate attention and retention;
- separate physical evidence from interpretation and metaphor.

## Allowed final visual language
- stylized history-fantasy illustration
- painterly cinematic animation
- graphic bronze / charcoal / ink treatment
- sculptural stylization
- 2.5D / 3D hybrid animation
- editorial-symbolic spaces
- bold designed lighting and depth

## Prohibited final visual language
- photorealistic / photo-realistic
- hyperreal / ultra-realistic
- live-action
- documentary reenactment
- realistic cinematic reconstruction
- "looks like a real photograph"
- skin-pore / lens realism as a quality goal

## Reference rule
Real photos, scans, museum images, excavation images, and anatomical evidence may be used only as:
- FACT_REFERENCE
- SHAPE_REFERENCE
- PROPORTION_REFERENCE

They may never become STYLE_REFERENCE unless explicitly transformed into a non-photographic style anchor.

## Unknown-detail rule
Unknown or disputed historical detail must be:
- omitted;
- simplified;
- silhouetted;
- abstracted;
- symbolically represented;
- separated into editorial space.

Never invent detailed realism to make the frame feel more authentic.

## Kinetic directing
The default attention engine is animation-space camera design:
- strong parallax
- spatial dive
- orbit / arc
- scale explosion / compression
- graphic match
- foreground wipe
- axial acceleration
- perspective reorientation
- speed contrast
- focus redirection

Use spectacular motion with one clear narrative function per beat. Variety matters more than stacking moves.

# Attention and Pacing

Viewer attention and retention are first-class production goals.

## Defaults
- meaningful change within 3-4 seconds when possible
- avoid 5-6 seconds of identical composition/focus without a reason
- 8s+ clips should usually have a second attention event around 4-6s
- avoid repeated slow push-in
- avoid static face enlargement as the only tension device
- avoid excessive 2-second cutting that destroys TTS continuity

## Attention Events
- reveal
- subject action
- parallax change
- focus shift
- spatial discovery
- camera reorientation
- meaningful light change
- transition cue


## Structural Limits
- 4-7s clip: 2-3 camera phases
- 8-12s clip: 3-5 camera phases
- <=15s clip: maximum 5 meaningful phases
- 1 primary camera move + at most 1 secondary adjustment
- do not combine 3 or more major move types in one clip unless explicitly justified

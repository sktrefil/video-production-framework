# History / Fantasy Boundary

## FACT_LOCK
Facts that must not be rewritten or invented:
- dates / ranges
- artifact type
- known site/location
- documented biological/genetic measurements
- documented existence/non-existence
- source-backed physical attributes

## FANTASY_ALLOWED
Allowed only when it does not become a false factual claim:
- cinematic atmosphere
- fog/weather
- restrained symbolic lighting
- camera choreography
- non-verbal visual metaphor
- clearly editorial explanatory space/panel
- tension-enhancing staging

## INVENTION_PROHIBITED
Do not fabricate:
- unsupported personal names
- family relationships
- undocumented meetings
- battles or violence
- causal motives stated as fact
- fictional artifact functions
- unsupported armor/weapons/writing/maps

When a fantasy device could be mistaken for physical evidence, separate it spatially and visually from the reconstructed historical environment.


## NON_REALISTIC FINAL-STYLE RULE

Historical evidence can constrain shape, chronology, geography, artifact type, and known physical facts without forcing a photorealistic reconstruction.

Use this split:
- KNOWN / SOURCE-BACKED -> preserve factual anchor.
- UNKNOWN / DISPUTED -> stylize, abstract, silhouette, omit, or separate into editorial space.
- NEVER convert missing knowledge into faux-documentary certainty.

Real evidence references are inputs, not final-style targets.
The finished frame must remain visibly stylized and non-photographic.

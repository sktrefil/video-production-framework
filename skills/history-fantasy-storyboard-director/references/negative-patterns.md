# Negative Patterns

Block or revise:
- poster-like centered static staging without purpose
- repeated WIDE + slow push
- decorative clutter blocking camera corridor
- background detail competing with the subject
- unsupported weapons/armor/text/maps
- physicalized editorial diagrams inside real anatomy/terrain
- overcomplicated camera move stacks
- identical camera speed for the full clip without narrative reason
- hard energy drop between adjacent clips without design intent
- reference-style drift overriding character/world continuity
- excessive texture/detail that reduces motion readability
